Important Note Regarding Antivirus & SmartScreen

Because NexusIDM uses low-level multi-threaded socket splitting and requests automatic UAC Administrator privileges on startup, Windows Defender or third-party suites may occasionally flag custom binaries as a "False Positive". If your antivirus blocks the application, please add an exclusion or temporarily allow the file to let the 32-stream engine operate at peak performance.
